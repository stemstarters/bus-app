How it works

Set it up in minutes. Run dismissal in seconds.

No apps to install, no student rosters to import. If a screen can open a web page, it can run Dismissal Magic.

1

Set up your school

Add your buses by color or number, choose how you call grades (like K–1, then 2–3, then 4–5), and pick your colors and sound.

2

Tap when a bus arrives

Whoever runs dismissal marks buses as they pull in and calls each grade group with one tap. Backups sign in with your school's PIN.

3

Classrooms light up

Every classroom screen shows a big bus pop-up and a chime for its group. Teachers send riders down—no interruptions over the intercom.

FAQ

Questions schools ask

Do teachers need an account or login?

No. Teachers open your school's classroom link once, pick their grade group, and leave it up. That's it.

What screens does it work on?

Anything with a web browser: interactive boards, classroom TVs, Chromebooks, laptops and tablets. There's nothing to install.

Who can call buses?

Anyone with your School Dashboard PIN—so a sub, office aide or assistant principal can run dismissal when you're out. You can change the PIN anytime.

What if the internet hiccups?

Classroom screens keep checking in on their own. If a screen loses its connection, a small dot turns orange, and it catches up as soon as it's back online.

What if our school's web filter blocks it?

Some school networks block websites until they've been reviewed. If a classroom screen can't open Dismissal Magic, ask your IT team to allow dismissalmagic.com and app.dismissalmagic.com. It's a one-time change, and we're happy to send them anything they need: hello@dismissalmagic.com.

Do you collect any student information?

No. Dismissal Magic stores your buses, grade groups and times—never student names, rosters or photos.

Can we use it for car riders and walkers?

Today Dismissal Magic is built for bus calls. Car rider calls (Carpool Magic) and walker calls are on the way, and founding schools help decide what comes next.

What happens after the free month?

Subscribe to keep going. If you don't, your setup is saved, so you can pick up right where you left off whenever you're ready.
